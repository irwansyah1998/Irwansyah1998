06 / How I think

Pragmatic by
design.

Good engineering isn’t complexity for its own sake. It is the judgment to understand the constraints and build what the situation actually needs.

  1. 01

    Start with the work

    Understand people, operations, data, and the environment before deciding what to build.

  2. 02

    Correctness before spectacle

    Reliable behavior, validation, and data integrity are part of the product — not an afterthought.

  3. 03

    Choose proportionate architecture

    Prefer clear, modular systems; avoid dependencies and abstractions that do not solve a real problem.

  4. 04

    Design with security in mind

    Consider trust boundaries, access, and operational risk while shaping a system, not after launch.

  5. 05

    Keep resources in view

    Performance, maintainability, and the cost of running software belong in engineering decisions.
